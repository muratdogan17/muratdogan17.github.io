How It Works

1

Add Players

Enter at least 2 player names and pick a difficulty level.

2

Pass the Phone

Hand the device to the current player. They see the target time and tap when ready.

3

The Timer Hides

The timer is visible for 1.5 seconds, then disappears. Now you're blind — trust your instincts.

4

Stop!

Tap the big red button when you think you've hit the target. The closer you are, the higher you rank.

5

See Who Wins

After everyone plays, the leaderboard reveals the rankings. Play again with a new random target!
