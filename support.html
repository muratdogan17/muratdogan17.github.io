Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play Blind Timer?
Add at least 2 players, pick a difficulty, and tap Start Game. Each player takes a turn trying to stop a hidden timer as close to the target time as possible. The player closest to the target wins the round.
What's the difference between Easy, Medium, and Hard?
Easy mode (2-5 seconds) gives you haptic vibration every second as a guide. Medium (5-10 seconds) and Hard (10-15 seconds) have no haptics — you rely entirely on your internal sense of time.
How do I restore my Premium purchase?
Go to Settings within the app and tap "Restore Purchases". If you purchased Premium on another device using the same Apple ID, your purchase will be restored.
What does Premium include?
Premium is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited players (free is limited to 4), removes all ads, and gives you unlimited game history.
How do I remove ads?
Upgrade to Premium from the Settings screen to permanently remove all ads.
Can I change the app language?
Yes. Go to Settings and select your preferred language. Blind Timer currently supports English and Turkish.
How do I clear my game history?
Go to Settings and tap "Clear Game History". This will permanently delete all saved rounds from your device.
Is my data shared with anyone?
No. Player names and game history are stored only on your device and are never sent to any server. Please see our Privacy Policy for details on third-party services used in the app.
