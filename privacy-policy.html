Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 17, 2026

Blind Timer ("the App") is a time estimation game developed by Murat Doğan ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the App and how it is used.

Information We Collect

The App itself does not require you to create an account, and we do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information such as your name, email address, or location. Player names entered during gameplay are stored only on your device and are never sent to any server.

Advertising

The free version of the App displays ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use certain data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads, including:

  • Device advertising identifier (IDFA)
  • General device information (model, OS version)
  • IP address (used to estimate general location for ad relevance)

When you first launch the App, you will be asked whether you allow tracking for personalized ads via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, only non-personalized ads will be shown.

For more information on how Google handles data, please review the Google Privacy Policy.

In-App Purchases

The App offers a one-time premium upgrade processed through Apple's App Store and managed by RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives a random anonymous user identifier and your purchase receipt from Apple to manage your entitlement. No personal information is shared with RevenueCat.

For more information, please review the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted:

  • Player names entered during game setup
  • Game history and round results
  • Language preference

You can delete all game history at any time from the Settings screen within the App.

Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since no personal data is collected from any user, the App complies with applicable children's privacy regulations.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob - Advertising
  • RevenueCat - Purchase management

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
